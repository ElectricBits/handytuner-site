Privacy Policy

ElectricBits Agent · last updated October 12, 2026

ElectricBits Agent is a private tool made and used by one person, Electric (ElectricBits), to look at the statistics of his own social media accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

Only the accounts Electric connects himself: public profile info (name, avatar), follower and like counts, and the list of his own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

Where the data goes

Nowhere else. It is stored only on Electric's own computers at home. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or sent to any third party.

What it does not do

It does not post, comment, follow, message or change anything on any account, and it does not read anyone else's account.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the platform's app settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Stored data is deleted when Electric stops using the tool.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ElectricBits/HandyTuner.
