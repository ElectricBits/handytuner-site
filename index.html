Features

Tuning app made for Android handhelds • Beta • Free & open source • No root • No ads • No tracking

Live HUD

Keep FPS, temperatures and battery visible while you play — useful information, not a wall of numbers.

Quick Menu

Open the controls over your game. Change a preset, cap frames or adjust power without breaking your flow.

Game presets

Give demanding games room to run and lighter games a battery-friendly profile. HandyTuner applies the right setup automatically.

Battery care

Set a charge limit and keep an eye on power draw, so long sessions are easier on the battery.

Low Latency mode

Wi-Fi only: stops the radio napping between packets and pauses background scans that cause lag spikes.

Network test

Measure your connection in about a minute, and see whether Low Latency mode helps on your own Wi-Fi.

Made for Android handhelds • Community driven

Built in

Meet the PULSE engine.

Performance tuning that reacts to the game instead of forcing you to babysit sliders.

AutoTDP

Adjusts power around your frame-rate target, helping avoid wasted watts.

Fan control

Balance cooling and noise from the same overlay you use to tune performance.

Frame caps

Choose a sensible ceiling for smoother pacing and lower power draw.

One control loop

Fewer tweak tools fighting over the same device settings.
